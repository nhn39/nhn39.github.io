About Me

My name is Nam Nguyen. I received my DMA in piano performance from Temple University in 2021. My dissertation, "Solo Piano Music by Vietnamese Composers: A Performative and Historical Perspective," explores the rich tapestry of Vietnamese piano repertoire through both a performative and historical lens. My principal teachers have included Ha Thu Tran, Harvey Wedeen, and Ching-Yun Hu. Additionally, I studied collaborative piano with Lambert Orkis and early keyboard instruments with Joyce Lindorff.

I began my piano training at the age of six at the Vietnam Academy of Music before coming to Temple. Despite my early exposure to piano in Vietnam, I had limited experience with classical music by Vietnamese composers and was unfamiliar with many works in the repertoire. The development of Classical Music in America, highlighted by innovative composers such as Charles Ives and George Gershwin, ignited my interest in my own culture. This led me to research Vietnamese piano music for my dissertation. I am continuing my research on Vietnamese music beyond the scope of my dissertation through my work with the Center for Vietnamese Philosophy, Culture, and Society.

Currently based in Philadelphia, I am working as a professional musician, engaging in both solo and collaborative performances. In addition to performing, I am passionate about teaching and has been instructing students of various ages and skill levels. Whether through performance, teaching, or collaboration, Nam is committed to enriching the musical landscape with his unique perspectives and expertise.
